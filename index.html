What cheap wiring and inverters really cost you

Fire & overheating risk

Undersized cable run hot under continuous solar load. Cheap wiring skips the safety margin that keeps insulation from degrading and connections from arcing.

Energy you paid for, lost

Every metre of thin, low-grade cable adds voltage drop. That's generation you paid for that never reaches your load — quietly stretching your payback period.

Inverters that die early

Unbranded inverters with weak MPPT tracking run hotter and fail years before a Tier 1 unit — usually right after the seller's short warranty runs out.

Voided warranties

Panel and inverter warranties are commonly conditional on correctly rated Balance of System components. Cut corners on cabling and you can lose that cover entirely.

Erratic, falling output

Fixed-tracking, budget inverters can't follow a real irradiance curve through the day, so yield swings and trends downward faster than the panels themselves age.

A second install bill

Re-wiring and re-inverting a system after five years costs more than specifying it correctly the first time — plus the roof labour you're paying for twice.
